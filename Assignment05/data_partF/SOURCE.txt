Part F sources (both downloaded 28 October 2026)

DATA: Our World in Data, COVID-19 dataset, file owid-covid-latest.csv
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/owid/covid-19-data/master/public/data/latest/owid-covid-latest.csv
  Columns used: iso_code, location, total_deaths, population, last_updated_date.
  Deaths: WHO COVID-19 dashboard (as compiled by OWID). Population: UN World Population Prospects.
  Data current to 2024-08-04 (India 2024-08-12).
  Mathieu, E. et al. (2020), "Coronavirus Pandemic (COVID-19)", OurWorldInData.org.

BOUNDARIES: Natural Earth, 1:50m Cultural Vectors, Admin 0 - Countries (de facto boundaries)
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson
  Repository VERSION file: 5.2.0-pre. Public domain. naturalearthdata.com
  Note: Natural Earth draws de facto control lines; other sources (GADM, OSM, national
  surveys) draw disputed areas differently.
