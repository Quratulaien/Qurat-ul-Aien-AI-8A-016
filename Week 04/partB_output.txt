mean removes the day-of-week pattern completely; 28 and 91 remove it too, but

large. The cost is 3 days at each end; 28 and 91 would cost 14 and 45.

Lie Factor = (effect shown) / (effect in the data). It needs ONE effect in the
data, measured between two fixed points that were chosen before looking. The
cherry-picked window has no single effect: each start and length gives a
different one, and which one is 'the data' depends on the choice being
judged. The formula assumes the comparison was declared first; with a
selected window that assumption is false, so the ratio has no value to take.
Honest substitute: fix the window before plotting, and report the whole
distribution of outcomes (every start, every length) rather than the one that
was selected. Here the distribution is the result.
